# Mutual NDA Draft — Latakia Coastal Land

> **Working draft only.** This text must be reviewed and adapted by qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdictions before signature. It does not create a binding agreement by itself.

## Parties

- **Disclosing / coordinating party:** AssetVeyra and/or the authorized seller, as identified in the signature version.
- **Receiving party:** The investor, developer or adviser identified in the signature version.

## Purpose

The parties may exchange confidential information solely to evaluate a potential hotel, resort or tourism development involving the Latakia Coastal Land opportunity, reference `latakia-coastal-land`.

## Confidential information

Confidential information includes non-public title, planning, legal, technical, commercial, contact and transaction information shared in the controlled Data Room or directly by an authorized party. Public information and information independently lawfully obtained are excluded to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## Use and access

The recipient will use the information only for the stated evaluation, limit access to representatives with a need to know, protect it with reasonable safeguards, and not contact the seller or authorities directly without the agreed authorization path.

## No representation

Information is provided for evaluation and remains subject to verification. No statement in the Data Room is a warranty of title, planning permission, value, return, availability or transaction completion.

## No obligation

The NDA does not require either party to proceed, make an offer or complete a transaction. Any transaction requires separate definitive documents.

## Return / deletion and term

On request, confidential information must be returned or deleted subject to legal, audit and backup retention requirements. The signature version should specify the confidentiality term, governing law, permitted disclosures and remedies.
