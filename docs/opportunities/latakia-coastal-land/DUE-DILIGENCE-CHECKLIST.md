# Due-Diligence Checklist — Latakia Coastal Land

Use this checklist to move the opportunity from `unverified` toward a documented verification decision. The checklist does not replace a licensed Syrian lawyer, surveyor, planner, tax adviser or sanctions/compliance professional.

| ID | Workstream | Required evidence | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| title-01 | Ownership | Current green title deed and cadastral extract | Seller | Open |
| title-02 | Ownership | Seller identity, authority, encumbrances and liens | Local counsel | Open |
| planning-01 | Planning | Written confirmation of commercial zoning and permitted hospitality use | Planner / authority | Open |
| planning-02 | Planning | Density, height, setbacks, coastal and environmental restrictions | Planner | Open |
| site-01 | Site | Boundary survey, coordinates and area measurement | Surveyor | Open |
| site-02 | Site | Confirmation of direct sea frontage and legal access | Surveyor / counsel | Open |
| utilities-01 | Infrastructure | Water, power, sewage, telecom and road access evidence | Seller / engineer | Open |
| legal-01 | Legal | Transferability, restrictions, taxes and transaction structure | Local counsel | Open |
| compliance-01 | Compliance | Sanctions, AML/KYC, beneficial ownership and cross-border review | AssetVeyra / counsel | Open |
| commercial-01 | Commercial | Independent valuation and feasibility assumptions | Adviser | Open |
| commercial-02 | Commercial | Development concept, capex, operating model and market evidence | Developer / adviser | Open |

## Release gates

- **Draft:** intake recorded; no investor distribution.
- **Verification:** documents and field checks are being reviewed; label as “verification in progress”.
- **Controlled investor package:** only after internal approval of the shareable summary, NDA and access rules.
- **Verified within documented scope:** only after authorized reviewers record the exact scope, evidence and limitations.
