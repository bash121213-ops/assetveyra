## Handling rules

- Do not upload identity documents or bank details to a public area.
- Use document version, uploader, checksum and review status for every file.
- Keep seller private notes and internal compliance findings outside investor-visible folders.
- A file being present means **received**, not **verified**.
- Access should be granted only to qualified users after the applicable NDA and workflow gate.
